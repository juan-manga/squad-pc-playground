# Product scope (Maintenance mode intake)

Generated by `squad optimize intake` (Squad Optimized — Plain Concepts). The triage agent uses
this file to decide what the business may request directly and what needs an engineer.

## Business may request (agents implement)

- UX and copy changes
- Workflows and screen flows
- Branding and theming
- Content
- Business rules and formulas

## Blocked for business requests (needs a maintainer), even when clear, small or testable

- Architecture, infrastructure, CI/CD
- Security, authentication, authorization
- Data models, migrations, public APIs
- Dependencies and licensing
- Anything under `.github/`

## Sizing

- Stories of **8 points or more** are split into children of 3–5 points before implementation.
